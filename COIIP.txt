COIIP — FINAL LOCKED MASTER SPECIFICATION
Project Name

COIIP – Community Operations & Incident Intelligence Platform

Tagline

Turning Incidents into Actionable Intelligence

Project Vision

COIIP is a multi-tenant cloud-hosted SaaS platform that helps organizations manage incidents, operations, service requests, assets, monitoring, governance, analytics, and AI-assisted decision-making from a single platform.

It is designed to simulate a real-world operations platform used by schools, colleges, hospitals, NGOs, housing societies, startups, corporate offices, municipalities, and government departments.

The project is specifically intended to demonstrate skills relevant to:

Cloud Support Associate
Infrastructure Operations
Technical Operations
System Administration
IT Operations
NOC Operations
Cloud Operations
Core Idea

Instead of being just a complaint portal, COIIP combines:

Incident Management
Service Request Management
Asset Management
Change Management
SLA Management
Escalation Management
Monitoring
Audit Logging
Governance
Operational Analytics
AI Assistance
Organizational Memory
Cloud Infrastructure Operations

into one unified SaaS platform.

Multi-Tenant Architecture

Each organization operates independently.

Examples:

School A cannot see School B data.
Hospital A cannot see Municipality B data.
Company A cannot see NGO B data.

Each organization receives:

Organization ID
Organization Admin Account
